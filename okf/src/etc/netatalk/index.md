# src/etc/netatalk

* [afp_avahi.c](afp_avahi.c.md) - Avahi based Zeroconf support.
* [afp_avahi.h](afp_avahi.h.md) - Avahi service registration interface and context type.
* [afp_mdns.c](afp_mdns.c.md) - mDNS based Zeroconf support
* [afp_mdns.h](afp_mdns.h.md) - mDNS service registration interface
* [afp_zeroconf.c](afp_zeroconf.c.md) - Register and withdraw AFP services through the Zeroconf implementation.
* [afp_zeroconf.h](afp_zeroconf.h.md) - Zeroconf service types and backend registration interface.
* [netatalk.c](netatalk.c.md) - The netatalk service controller that starts and supervises afpd and the Spotlight dbus daemon.
